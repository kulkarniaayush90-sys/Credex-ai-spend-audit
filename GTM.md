# GTM

## Positioning

Credex AI Spend Audit is a free top-of-funnel product that helps startups prove immediate value before a sales conversation.

## Funnel

1. User lands on the audit page from founder content, AI tooling posts, or product-led SEO
2. They enter their AI spend profile and receive a shareable report
3. If the report shows meaningful savings, the CTA nudges them toward a Credex conversation
4. Lead capture gathers email, company, role, and team size
5. Resend confirmation keeps the interaction feeling polished and credible

## Distribution Angles

- Product Hunt launch
- Founder-led LinkedIn content
- SEO pages for AI spend reduction and software subscription audits
- Partnership with startup accelerators and finance communities
