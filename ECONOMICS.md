# Economics

## Product Economics

The product is intentionally lightweight to operate:

- Audit computation is local and deterministic
- AI usage is only for a short summary
- Public report storage is a single JSON payload per audit
- Lead capture volumes should remain low enough for modest Supabase and Resend usage

## Unit Economics Assumptions

- One audit typically costs well under $0.05 in infrastructure when Anthropic is not called
- AI summary generation is the main variable cost
- The product is designed to qualify leads rather than maximize free usage volume

## Revenue Logic

- The audit creates immediate value by surfacing waste
- Larger savings reports justify a stronger sales CTA
- Smaller savings reports still create trust and route users into a renewal review conversation
