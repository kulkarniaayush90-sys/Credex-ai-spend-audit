# User Interviews

## Interview Themes

### Startup Founders

- Want quick answers, not a lengthy procurement process
- Care about whether spend is obviously wasteful
- Prefer honesty over exaggerated ROI claims

### Engineering Leaders

- Want the recommendation logic to feel defensible
- Need seat counts and plan tiers translated into practical next steps
- Appreciate a shareable report they can forward internally

### Finance Operators

- Focus on renewal timing, duplicate tools, and unused seats
- Want annualized savings clearly surfaced
- Prefer a clean audit trail over clever marketing language
